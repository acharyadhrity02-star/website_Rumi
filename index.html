Professional Summary

Postdoctoral Associate at the School of Integrative Engineering, Chung-Ang University (Seoul), working on the development of multifunctional biomaterials for antimicrobial therapy and tissue regeneration.

Multidisciplinary researcher specializing in bioactive and electro-magnetic hydrogel systems for regenerative medicine, smart skin patches, and biosensing. Skilled in 3D bioprinting, nanocomposite design, microneedle fabrication, and advanced cellular assays (hBMSCs, HaCaT).

Experienced in GLP documentation, ISO 13485 QMS environments, and data-driven scientific communication, with publications in Advanced Healthcare Materials, Advanced Materials Technologies, Biomaterials, and the Journal of Nanobiotechnology.

Key R&D Projects

Bioactive Ion-Reinforced Bio-Ceramic Ink

Shear-thinning hybrid ink integrating bio-ceramics with bioactive ions and polymeric binders for high-fidelity DIW printing and improved osteogenic/antibacterial performance.

Mg–Nanodiamond–HAp 3D-Printed Scaffold

Ion-releasing hybrid scaffold with controlled Mg²⁺ release (ICP-OES) and osteogenic validation (ALP / ARS / qPCR) for bone tissue engineering.

mCNC Conductive Microneedle Hydrogel

Dual-stimulus platform enhancing ZO-1 / YAP-TAZ pathways for skin barrier restoration, with tunable rheology for smart skin patch applications.

Piezoelectric ZnO–Mg²⁺ Bioglass Scaffold

Piezoelectric-assisted bone regeneration with angiogenic enhancement for advanced tissue engineering applications.
